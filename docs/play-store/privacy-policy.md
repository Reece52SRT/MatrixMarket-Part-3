# Matrix Market - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Matrix Market ("the app") is a student project marketplace. This policy explains what we collect and why.

**What we collect**
- Account details: your name, student email address and (optionally) campus.
- Listings you create, including photos you choose to upload.
- Offers, trade meetings and your Student Score.
- A push-notification token for your phone, so we can alert you about offers and meetings.

**What we don't collect**
- We never see your fingerprint or face. Biometric unlock is handled entirely by your phone.
- We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.

**How it is stored**
- Data is sent over HTTPS to our API hosted on Microsoft Azure (South Africa North region).
- Passwords are stored only as secure one-way hashes (BCrypt).
- Listings you browse may be saved on your phone so the app works offline; logging out clears them.

**Your choices**
- Turn notifications off at any time in Settings.
- Delete your own listings from the listing screen.
- To delete your account, contact the team at the email on the store listing.
