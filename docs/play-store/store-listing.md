## Short description (max 80 characters)
Buy, sell, lend & trade textbooks and gear with verified students on campus.

## Full description
Matrix Market is the campus marketplace built for students.

- **Verified students only** - sign up with your student email or Google.
- **Scan to sell** - scan the barcode on a textbook and Matrix Market fills in the title for you.
- **Add photos** so buyers know exactly what they're getting.
- **Safe meet-ups** - pick a verified campus spot (library foyer, campus security lobby...). A 6-digit code confirms the hand-over so nobody gets scammed.
- **Student Score & trust badges** - every completed trade builds your reputation.
- **Works offline** - browse saved listings and create new ones without signal; they upload automatically when you're back online.
- **Instant notifications** for new offers, meetings and completed trades.
- **Fingerprint / face unlock** for quick, secure access.
- Available in **English, Afrikaans and isiZulu**.
